HIDDEN MOTION

Four moments in the app. Each loops here; in the product each fires once.
DROP
The file comes apart into ink and pools into its handle.
READY
Processing dots ripple, then pull together with a small burst.
BREATH
Idle mark. A tentacle twitches now and then. Hover to scatter.
EMPTY, ALIVE
Ink grows slowly under the drop zone. Hover leaves a trail.
