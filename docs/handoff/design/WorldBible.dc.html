THE WORLD

Draft. Yours to rewrite.
THE NOSY ONES

Eyeballs. Lots of them. Not evil, just insatiable. They want to see everything, every which way, all the time. They dart, they stare, they never blink long enough.

They stand in for every model, log and server that would love a look at your W-2.

THE OCTOPUS

Quiet, calm, a little smug. It doesn't fight. It inks. It hands your agent what it needs and keeps the rest under its arms.

It stands in for you, on your own machine.

THE INK

The only weapon. Wherever it pools, the eyes go dark. When it disperses, something is moving.

It's the redaction, the loader and the logo, all made of the same dots.

RULES
  1. Everything is 3D, rendered down to dots. Nothing is flat-drawn.
  2. Eyes are light. Ink is dark. The eyes live in the dark; the app lives on paper.
  3. The octopus never attacks an eye. It only blocks the view.
  4. Eyes always look at something. If nothing is happening, they look at you.
  5. Big motion only when something changes. At rest, the world barely breathes.
WHERE THEY SHOW UP

Onboarding: the eyes crowd your Downloads folder until the octopus moves in. Drop: eyes turn toward the file, ink closes over it. Agent read: one eye peeks, sees only ink, blinks. Empty state: a few eyes drifting in the dark, looking for something to look at.
